THE FORD SHELBY MUSTANG
GT350

The GT350 is a blend of history and high-performance engineering.

The GT350 nameplate has a rich history, dating back to the mid 1960s when Carroll Shelby transformed the Mustang into a track ready machine. The modern GT350 pays homage to this heritage while incorporating contemporary technology and performance enhancements.

Design and Features

Exterior

The GT350 features an aggressive and aerodynamic design, with a prominent front grille, hood vents, and a rear spoiler.

Interior

Inside, you'll find a driver-focused cockpit with Recaro sport seats, a flat-bottom steering wheel, and performance gauges.

Suspention

The car is equipped with a MagneRide adaptive suspension system, providing a smooth yet responsive ride.

Engine and Performance

The GT350 is equipped with a naturally aspirated 5.2-liter V8 engine known as the "Voodoo." This engineproduces around 526 horsepower and 429 lb-ft of torque.
